# Guardian is running on this computer

A parent set up Guardian on this PC. It watches the **{account}** account. This screen explains exactly what it does. Nothing is hidden.

## What Guardian records

- The name of the app you are using and how long you use it.
- Which websites you visit: the site name (like youtube.com) and the page title. Not the full address, and never what you type.
- The time of day, and whether the computer is idle or locked.

## What Guardian never records

- Keystrokes, passwords, or anything you type.
- Screenshots, screen recording, camera, or microphone.
- Messages, chats, email, or the contents of files.

## What can happen

- A warning 5 minutes and 1 minute before a limit or bedtime.
- Apps in a category may be closed when that category's time is up.
- At bedtime, or when the daily limit is reached, you are signed out (not shut down). Save your work when you see the warning.
- You can ask for more time from the Guardian tray icon; a parent decides.

## Where to see it yourself

- Open **{url}** on this computer to see your own activity, today's limits, and this week's schedule — the same numbers your parent sees.
- The Guardian icon is always in the tray while it is running. Right-click it for this text at any time.
- Supported browsers for website tracking: Chrome and Edge. Private/Incognito windows are turned off by policy.
